# Directory Submission Plan (Phase 20 Loop H4)

> Generated 2026-05-07 · Phase 20 Loop H · doc-only ship per UF directive

## Why directories matter for DPL

Directory presence is one of the few B2B service-firm signals that compounds over time without ongoing creative work: list once, get backlinks + intent traffic + cross-platform validation indefinitely. For an AI-automation managed service positioning at $50K-$100K retainer scale, the audience pre-qualifies on these directories before booking calls. Net effect for DPL: better-fit pipeline, fewer tire-kickers, and the side benefit of authority signals propagating into AI-search citation surfaces (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews).

## Tier 4 — AI-search-only signals (no direct submission)

These do not accept submissions. They surface DPL via the schema + content shipped on the site (Service schema, Person schema, Organization schema, BlogPosting schema all shipped in Phase 20 Loop A-G work). Their mention is the goal of the on-site SEO + AI-search work.

| Surface | What it cites |
|---|---|
| Perplexity | Cites pages with strong schema + clean attribution. Service / Person / Article schemas all help here. |
| ChatGPT | Cites pages with explicit factual claims + named-source attribution. The H6 design-only flag on /research is correct: AI-search prefers honest "no source yet" over fabricated numbers. |
| Google AI Overviews | Cites pages with FAQ schema + clear question-answer structure. /faq + per-page FAQ blocks already ship the schema. |
| Claude.ai | Cites pages with strong topical depth and on-page citations. /guides is the strongest surface. |
